Production Hours = kolom Lama Produksi (jam) per OKP di tab START-STOP. Uptime (jam) = Production Hours − Planned DT − Unplanned DT − Trial − OFF yang terjadi di antara Jam Mulai–Jam Akhir OKP itu. Uptime % = Uptime (jam) ÷ Production Hours. Output teoritis = kapasitas teoritis (kg/jam) × Uptime (jam); Performa = Total output (1st TECO + 2nd + Sample + Total Waste DATA ABI & RIAN) ÷ Output teoritis — sama dengan halaman OEE. Planned/Unplanned DT, Trial, dan OFF = kolom "in hours" di tab DOWNTIME (Time Category PDT / UPDT / TRIAL / OFF). Output 1st = kolom QTY STHP KG (kolom O) di tab TECO.
